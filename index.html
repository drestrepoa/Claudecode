Our framework

Four dimensions decide whether AI creates value

Most organisations have adopted AI. Few have decided what it should change. We diagnose and redesign across four dimensions, from where you are today to what must change.

S

Strategy

FromAn adoption programme counted in licences and logins.

ToExplicit choices on where AI competes, and a plan to capture the gain in margin, price and client value.

D

Data

FromDocuments and records scattered, ungoverned, hard to retrieve.

ToA governed body of knowledge, with owners, that AI can actually use.

T

Technology

FromEvery intelligent layer rented, on the vendors' terms and timetables.

ToA deliberate line: rent the models, own the knowledge, agents and apps that set you apart.

S

Skills

FromTraining as one-off events: valuable, voluntary, unmeasured.

ToRoles, competencies and incentives redesigned around the new way of working.

Start from the process, not the tool.The unit of change is a process from request to deliverable, not a job or a licence.
Let the client priority decide.Cost, speed, quality or risk: each process changes for a reason your clients value.
Decide who keeps the gain.Efficiency creates value only once you decide where it goes: margin, price or new services.

How we work

From decision to lasting value, in three phases

Each engagement is paced by your own calendar: budget cycles, vendor renewals and board decisions.

Phase 1

Decide and baseline

Confirm the strategic choices, select two or three processes, name their owners and measure where you start: effort, turnaround, quality and margin.

Phase 2

Pilot and measure

Redesign the selected processes with AI, run the pilots against the baseline and decide in advance where the freed capacity goes.

Phase 3

Scale and capture

Extend what works, set pricing and incentives, and build the operating model that keeps standards central while teams own their problems.
