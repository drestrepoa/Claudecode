Our framework

Four questions every AI strategy must answer

Technology alone rarely decides the outcome. We work through four connected dimensions, so that each choice supports the others.

Where will AI create an advantage for you, and how will you capture it?

We start from your markets, your customers and your economics, not from the tools. Together we choose where AI should compete for you and how the value it creates reaches your results.

We look at

  • Competitive position and customer needs
  • How you create, deliver and price value
  • Risks of standing still

You get

  • A clear AI ambition and positioning
  • A prioritised portfolio of opportunities
  • A roadmap the leadership can steer

What knowledge do you hold that no one else has?

Models are widely available; your data and expertise are not. We help you turn what your organisation knows into an asset that AI can use safely and well.

We look at

  • Where your critical knowledge lives
  • Quality, ownership and access
  • Privacy, confidentiality and compliance

You get

  • A governed, usable knowledge base
  • Clear data ownership
  • The foundations for reliable AI

What should you buy, and what should you build?

Not every capability deserves the same treatment. We help you decide where standard platforms are enough and where a solution of your own makes the difference.

We look at

  • Your current tools and vendors
  • Integration with your systems
  • Security, cost and dependency

You get

  • A deliberate technology architecture
  • A buy-or-build decision for each need
  • Solutions developed where they matter

Which skills, roles and incentives will make the change last?

Transformation succeeds when people see what is in it for them. We design the capabilities, the roles and the ways of working that let teams make AI their own.

We look at

  • Current skills and readiness
  • Roles, career paths and incentives
  • Culture and leadership

You get

  • A skills plan by role and level
  • Training that changes daily work
  • Leaders equipped to sponsor the change

Perspectives

What we believe about AI and strategy

Strategy

Usage is not value

Counting licences and logins tells you who uses AI, not whether it pays.

Read moreClose
Most organisations measure AI by how widely it is used. That is a useful start, but it says nothing about results. The question leaders need answered is different: what does the organisation now do better, faster or at lower cost, and where does that gain end up? Strategy begins when usage is connected to outcomes your clients and your board recognise.
Operations

Redesign the work, not the task

Speeding up one step often just moves the bottleneck to the next.

Read moreClose
When AI accelerates a single task, the time saved is often absorbed by review, rework or waiting further down the line. Lasting gains come from looking at the whole flow of work, from request to result, and asking what it would look like if it were designed today. That is where quality, speed and cost can improve together.
People

Change is a people project

Tools spread quickly. Habits, roles and incentives do not.

Read moreClose
Training sessions create curiosity; they rarely change how work is done. People adopt new ways of working when their roles, their objectives and their recognition move with them. We treat skills, incentives and leadership as part of the strategy, not as an afterthought to the technology.

Questions

Where do we start if we have already invested in AI tools?

With what you already have. We review how the tools are used, what they deliver and where the larger opportunities lie, then build the strategy on that foundation rather than starting from zero.

Do you only advise, or do you also build?

Both. Our work runs from strategy to implementation and, where standard solutions fall short, to developing processes, agents and tools tailored to your organisation.

How do you take regulation into account?

From the start. Regulatory foresight is part of our strategic work, including frameworks such as the EU AI Act and data protection rules, so that what you build is compliant by design.

How long does an engagement take?

It depends on your ambition and your starting point. We agree the scope together and design the first phase to reach decisions early, so momentum builds from the outset.
